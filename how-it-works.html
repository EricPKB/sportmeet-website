How it works

From swipe to squad, in six steps.

No dead group chats, no chasing people down. Here's the actual flow, screen by screen.

Step 1–2

Set up your profile once.

Add a photo, pick your sports and skill level, and set your general location and availability. This is what your matches see — and what Sportmeet uses to find the right people nearby.

Step 3–4

Swipe to match, then just talk.

Swipe by sport and skill level. When you both match, the chat opens right away — no waiting for approval, no separate app to switch to.

Step 5

Create a game, or join one nearby.

Set the sport, player count, place, and time — Sportmeet handles the rest. Or skip creating one entirely and join a game that's already forming near you on the map.
