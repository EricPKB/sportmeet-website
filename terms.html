Terms and Community Rules

Last updated: October 6, 2026

These Terms and Community Rules apply when you use the Sportmeet mobile app (the “App”), run by Sportmeet in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to them. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the App.

1. Who can use Sportmeet

  • You must be 18 or older. The App asks you to confirm this before you sign up, and tapping “I’m 18+ and I agree” also means you accept these Terms, the Community Rules and our Privacy Policy.
  • You sign in with a mobile phone number you control. Keep your account and verification codes to yourself; you’re responsible for activity on your account.
  • Give accurate profile information. Don’t pretend to be someone else, and don’t create accounts to get around a block, a report or a suspension.

2. Community Rules

Sportmeet is for meeting people to play sports. These rules apply to everything you post or send: profile name, bio and photos, game titles and places, and chat messages.

Do not post, send or do any of the following:

  • Harassment, bullying, stalking, intimidation or threats of any kind.
  • Hateful, abusive or discriminatory content or conduct, including attacks on people because of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar traits.
  • Sexual, nudity, sexually explicit or sexually suggestive content, or unwelcome sexual advances.
  • Violence, or content that encourages violence, self-harm or dangerous acts.
  • Illegal activity or content, including anything involving minors in a sexual or harmful way.
  • Impersonation, fake profiles, spam, scams, or advertising unrelated to Sportmeet.
  • Sharing someone else’s private information or photos without their permission.
  • Photos or content you don’t have the right to share.
  • Trying to break, overload, scrape or reverse-engineer the App, or to access other people’s accounts.

Respect other people’s boundaries. If someone asks you to stop contacting them, stop.

3. Reporting and blocking

  • Report a person or conversation using the ••• menu on their profile or in a chat. Choose a reason and, if you like, add details. You can also email support@sportmeet.io.
  • Block anyone from the same menu. Once someone is blocked, neither of you can send the other messages or game invitations, and you can’t match, until you unblock them in Profile → Block list. They no longer appear in your Discover deck. We don’t tell people they’ve been blocked or reported.
  • We run a basic automated check on names, bios, game details and messages and may refuse text that contains clearly prohibited language. It can’t catch everything, so please report anything that gets through. We do not automatically review photos.
  • Our team reviews reports manually. We don’t promise a response time, and we may not tell you what we decided. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

You can appeal or ask a question by emailing support@sportmeet.io.
