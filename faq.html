FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Is Sportmeet free?

Yes — Sportmeet is free to use. Optional premium features may be introduced later, but the core matching, chat, and game-creation experience stays free.

What cities is it available in?

Toronto first, expanding city by city from there. See the Cities page to tell us where you'd like Sportmeet to launch next.

How does matching actually work?

You swipe by sport and skill level, similar to a dating app. When you and someone else both swipe yes, a chat opens between you.

Is my phone number and data safe?

Your phone number is used to verify your account and is never shown publicly to other users. See our Safety & Trust page and Privacy Policy for full detail.

How do I report or block someone?

Both are available directly from a match or chat. Blocking is instant; reporting opens a short form so our team can follow up.

What if no one responds to my match?

It happens — try browsing games directly on the Map tab instead of waiting on a match, or create your own game and let players come to you.

How is this different from a Facebook group or Meetup?

Those are built around one-off events. Sportmeet is built around the people — matching by sport and skill so the same group can keep playing together, not just show up once.

How do I delete my account?

From Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. If you run into any issue, email support@sportmeet.io.
