Company summary

Sportmeet helps people build a network of friends to play sports with, using a dating-app-style swipe-to-match. Founded in Toronto in 2024 by Eric Bell (CEO) and Tharujan Ravitharan (CTO), both Toronto Metropolitan University Master of Digital Media graduates.
