How it works

Three steps from stranger to squad.

1

Match

Swipe by sport and skill level to find players near you — the same way you find anything else worth finding.

2

Chat

Message your match directly in the app. No waiting around, no dead group chats.

3

Play

Create a game or join one nearby. Add friends by phone number and turn one match into a squad that keeps showing up.

Who it's for

Built for anyone rebuilding their game, not just finding one.

Most tools help you find a one-off event. Sportmeet is built around the people — so the second game happens as naturally as the first.

  • Newcomers rebuilding a network in a new city
  • Recreational athletes looking to fill spots in their group/event
  • Busy professionals who want built-in accountability
  • Students looking for pickup games on campus
  • Adults easing back into sport
  • Anyone tired of dead rec sports group chats
